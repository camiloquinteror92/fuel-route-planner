Assignment defaults

Change the truck or the rules, then replan. A trip already planned is planned again on its cached route: no external call.

Miles driven on one gallon. Fewer miles per gallon means more gallons for the same trip.

How far a full tank goes. With the fuel economy it sets the tank: — (range ÷ mpg).

mi

How far from the road a station may be. Wider: more stations to choose from, and longer detours (the detour is not priced).

default —
gal

Fuel that must still be in the tank when the truck reaches any stop and the destination. None by default: the range is used to the last drop.

Price of a station quoted several times

The price file lists some stations more than once, with different prices. Median (default) ignores one odd quote; lowest and highest are the best and the worst case.

A stop that buys less than — is merged with a neighbour when that costs at most — more. Off: the pure cheapest plan, tiny stops included.
