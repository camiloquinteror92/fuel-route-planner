Where to buy fuel

—. At every truck stop, the driver looks one full tank ahead (—) and follows three rules:

No fuel stop needed: the fuel in the tank covers this trip.

  1. Cheaper ahead

    Is fuel cheaper further on, within reach? Buy just enough to get there.

  2. Finish

    If not, can the truck reach the finish? Buy just enough to finish.

  3. Fill up

    If neither, fill the tank here. Nothing within reach is cheaper.

Why the three rules give the cheapest plan: each gallon is bought at the cheapest truck stop that can still reach the mile where it is burned. A test compares the rules with a slow method that tries every possible amount at every truck stop, on many small made-up roads: the cost is always the same.
