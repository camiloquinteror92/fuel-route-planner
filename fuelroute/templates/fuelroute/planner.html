Why it is fast

  • No geocoding call. “City, ST” and “lat,lon” are resolved offline from an index of — places (Census and GeoNames).
  • One routing call, small. OSRM answers in polyline6, decoded locally, over an HTTPS connection that stays open between trips.
  • Two caches and single flight. The prepared route and the finished plan are cached (plan for —); identical requests in flight share one routing call.
  • Vectorised corridor search. numpy finds the stations near the route in three passes: see the funnel in How it works.
